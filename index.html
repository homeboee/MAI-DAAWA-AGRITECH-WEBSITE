Our home base · Kaduna State

Greener streets. Native plants. Stronger ecosystems.

From our base in Kurmin Mashi, Kaduna, Mai Daawa Agritech aims to support practical restoration that communities can sustain. We focus on urban tree planting and roadside greenery, while promoting native plant species that suit local conditions.

  • ✓Urban and roadside tree planting.
    Encourage suitable trees and greener verges with safe placement and ongoing care.
  • ✓Restore native plant species.
    Promote locally appropriate vegetation to support biodiversity and ecosystem stability.
  • ✓Community guidance on threatened species.
    Share awareness on limiting endangered plant use and considering sustainable substitutes.

We are building this work through community engagement and future activities. No completed projects or measured impact are claimed here.

Technology with purpose

Better tools for better-growing businesses.

Alongside our environmental focus, Mai Daawa Agritech creates practical digital tools that help agribusinesses manage information, make clearer decisions and work more sustainably.

  • ✓Automated Google Sheets and financial tracking logsCustom multi-tab spreadsheet templates for cash flow, inventory, expenses and day-to-day operating metrics for smallholder farmers and micro-agribusinesses.
  • ✓Interactive agribusiness dashboardsClear visual summaries of production costs, yields, stock movement, mortality and hatchability rates, and other key performance indicators. Data updates depend on the connected records and workflow.
  • ✓Sustainable digital productsLightweight digital workflows, web-to-sheet data collection systems and customer database structures for agricultural micro-enterprises and local retail businesses.
  • ✓Agribusiness data literacy trainingPractical guidance for rural agricultural enterprises and student entrepreneurs on digital record-keeping, spreadsheet use and data-informed management.
